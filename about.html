About FullShelf Medico Solutions Limited

Complete medical equipment solutions, from first assessment to lasting support.

Who we are

FullShelf Medico Solutions Limited is a full-cycle medical equipment partner for healthcare providers and families. We support you at every stage: advising on what you need, assessing your site, preparing specifications, procuring and supplying the right equipment, installing it, training your team, and keeping it running through maintenance.

Whether you are equipping a new clinic, upgrading a hospital department, restocking a pharmacy, or buying a wheelchair for a family member, you deal directly with our team from the first question to long-term support.
