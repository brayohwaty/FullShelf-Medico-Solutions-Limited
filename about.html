About FullShelf Medico Solutions Limited

A full shelf of healthcare, medical and laboratory products — supported from first enquiry to ongoing service.

Who we are

FullShelf Medico Solutions Limited is a healthcare supply and solutions partner serving hospitals, clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, healthcare projects and families. Our product scope extends beyond medical equipment to include medical consumables, laboratory reagents, test kits, laboratory equipment and supplies, hospital furniture, patient-care products and specialist clinical systems.

Whether you are planning an imaging or dialysis unit, equipping an operating theatre, setting up a laboratory, replenishing everyday consumables, sourcing reagents, furnishing a ward or choosing a mobility aid, our team helps you define the requirement, identify suitable options and coordinate supply. We also support site assessment, specifications, installation, training and maintenance where required.

What we do

Product sourcing and practical support across equipment, consumables and laboratory supply categories.
