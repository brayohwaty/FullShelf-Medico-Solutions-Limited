Our services

Practical sourcing, procurement and technical support across medical equipment, consumables, reagents and laboratory supplies.

Every stage, one team

From a single replenishment order to a complete facility or department project, use the support you need.

1

Product & project consultancy

Guidance on equipment, consumables, reagents and laboratory supplies to match clinical needs, workflows and budgets.

2

Site assessment

We review space, power, water, ventilation, medical-gas needs and workflow before equipment recommendations are made.

3

Specifications & planning

Clear technical and product specifications for equipment, consumables and laboratory requirements, supporting tenders and budgeting.

4

Procurement & multi-category supply

We source medical equipment, hospital furniture, consumables, reagents, laboratory supplies and specialist systems.

5

Installation and commissioning

Equipment set up correctly and tested before handover.

6

Training

Practical user training and handover support for supplied equipment, based on the agreed project scope.

8

Consumables & reagent replenishment

How it works

  1. Talk to usTell us what you are planning: a new clinic, a department upgrade, or a single item for home care.
  2. Assess and specifyWe assess your site and prepare clear specifications and recommendations.
  3. Quote and procureYou receive a clear quote. We source and supply the right equipment.
  4. Install and trainWe install and commission it, then train your team to use it safely.
  5. Maintain and supportServicing and repairs keep your equipment working for the long term.

Some installation and servicing work may be carried out with the equipment manufacturer or certified partners. We coordinate it so you deal with one team.
